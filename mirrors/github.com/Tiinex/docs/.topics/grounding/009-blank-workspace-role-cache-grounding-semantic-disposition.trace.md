# Blank-Workspace Role-Cache Grounding Semantic Disposition

Current Tiinex semantics are sufficient for fresh grounding from a blank/minimal selected Workspace plus exact qualified route-bounded cache/Role material. No Cache, Participant, Process-Applicability, Holder, Capability-Map or new readiness schema is required. The durable contract is a **forward-selected qualified semantic closure**: cache/representation qualification establishes exact material identity, integrity, provenance, availability and bounded coverage; the exact artifacts carried through that material retain only the semantics they already own; and current-work relevance, applicability, requiredness, participation, holder state and source authority must still come from explicit qualified controlling authority.

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: blank/minimal Workspace grounding from qualified route-bounded cache/Role material
- Decision: use existing Workspace representation, Handoff Required/Reference Context, Role, Task/Epic, Decision, typed Relation and exact pointer/reference semantics; treat cache as a material-resolution surface only; require forward current-authority selection for every stronger current-work claim; add no new canonical schema family or readiness lifecycle state.

### Three layers that must remain separate

1. **Representation/material layer** — a qualified complete Workspace, bounded Workspace representation, detached/cache entry, Role-grounding pointer or exact reference may establish that exact material is available/resolvable, where it came from, what bytes/digest qualified, and what coverage boundary applies. This layer does not itself establish applicability, participation, holder identity, delegation, source mutation authority, process requiredness or completion.
2. **Artifact-owned semantic layer** — once exact cached material resolves to a qualified Role, Decision, Relation, Process, Task, Handoff, Workspace or other semantic artifact, that artifact retains the semantics it already owns. Cache carriage neither weakens nor strengthens those semantics. A cached Role may therefore establish its own Role label, boundary, `May Do`, `Does Not Authorize`, and Holder Relationship; a cached Decision may establish its decision; a cached Relation may establish its typed edge; a cached process may establish its reusable procedure/trigger meaning. These facts are authoritative because of the qualified artifact, not because they are cached.
3. **Current-work binding layer** — whether one of those artifacts is relevant to the selected work, whether a process applies or is mandatory, whether a Role is a relevant participant/capability, whether a session is authorized to hold the Role, and whether source mutation/creation is authorized must be established by explicit qualified current-scope authority and an exact forward selector from the selected Handoff/current frontier/Role/Decision. Cache membership, nearby filenames, sibling artifacts and reverse relation discovery never fill this layer.

This separation is the acceptance contract for both complete and blank/minimal Workspace shapes. A full repository merely makes more material available; it does not change the resolution rule.

### Exact cache and Workspace interpretation

A qualified Workspace dependency/cache may establish only the following **by virtue of its representation status**:

- exact material identity or selector target;
- exact qualified bytes/digest when carried;
- source Workspace/repository/ref/root identity when the owning Workspace artifact declares it;
- representation class such as complete Workspace, bounded representation, qualified detached/cache material, exact pointer/reference, or unavailable authority;
- coverage boundary and omission semantics;
- material availability and provenance sufficient to explain how an exact target was resolved.

Omitted cache entries are unknown/outside the represented scope, not proven absent from the source Workspace. A bounded/cache representation must not be promoted to complete because all currently requested entries resolved. Detached cached bytes must not be silently rewritten as Workspace membership unless exact source authority says they are part of that Workspace.

A cached artifact may support a stronger semantic claim only when all of the following hold:

1. the exact artifact is independently qualified as the schema/semantic owner of that claim;
2. qualified current authority forward-selects that artifact or an exact authority chain containing it for the bounded work; and
3. the artifact's own scope/effect actually states the claimed meaning.

The stronger claim therefore belongs to the semantic artifact and current-work binding, never to cache presence itself.

### Exact forward rule for process applicability and requiredness

The accepted Process Applicability disposition remains controlling. Blank/minimal Workspace delivery changes only how the exact material is resolved, not what makes a process apply.

A cold recipient may resolve process semantics only by this forward procedure:

1. start from the exact selected Handoff, explicit current Role binding and qualified current-work frontier;
2. inspect only process declarations/selectors directly present in those controlling artifacts and their exact Required Context, qualified pointers, or route-bounded cache selectors;
3. follow only the exact selected authority chain needed for the claim — for example the process procedure, an operative adoption Decision, an applicability Relation, or a standing Role obligation;
4. classify **availability**, **applicability**, **requiredness**, **active execution**, **ownership/responsibility**, and **completion evidence** independently from the exact declaration that owns each dimension; and
5. when a necessary selector, authority artifact or exact material target is missing/unavailable/ambiguous, preserve that dimension as unresolved and emit the exact missing-authority/material blocker. Do not reverse-scan Relations, enumerate process inventory, inspect unselected cache neighbors, or search a repository/connector as substitute authority.

Accepted forward declaration shapes remain:

- **Direct current-work invocation** — the controlling Task/Epic/Handoff/Decision/Role names the exact process, the bounded scope in which it applies, and any mandatory/ownership effect that matters. When the process bytes live only in cache, Required Context or another exact qualified pointer selects that cached process material.
- **Adoption plus applicability Relation** — an operative Decision owns process adoption/effect and a typed Relation binds it to the exact target scope. A cold target-side recipient must receive a forward selector to that chain from the selected Handoff/current work; cache presence of the Relation/Decision/process without that selector is insufficient.
- **Standing Role obligation** — the selected Role itself explicitly names the process as a standing obligation for its bounded Role scope. The selected Handoff resolves the exact Role and the exact process authority through Required Context/pointer/cache material as needed.

`Handoff -> Required Context` establishes that the selected material is required to understand/perform the transfer. It does **not** by itself establish that a referenced process is applicable or mandatory. If the Handoff itself says `use/require process X for this transfer`, that direct invocation supplies the applicability/requiredness claim and Required Context supplies the material closure.

### Recipient Role and Role-cache boundary

Recipient Role grounding is a special **selection** case, not a participant shortcut:

- the selected Handoff `To: <Role>` identifies the bounded recipient capacity;
- exact qualified Role material, whether from a complete Workspace or route-bounded Role cache, may establish recipient Role compatibility and the Role's intrinsic capability/authority/holder-assignment semantics;
- an explicit current-session Role-binding assertion remains separate runtime provenance;
- qualified Holder Relationship / Current Holder / Relation / Decision authority must authorize that binding mode;
- durable Party/person/model holder identity remains unresolved unless exact holder authority establishes it.

Neither the Handoff endpoint nor the Role cache entry is a semantic participant declaration. The recipient Role may be the active transfer capacity while `participantMap` remains not-established.

For any additional cached Role:

- availability plus the Role artifact may establish **what that Role is and what it may do**;
- it does not establish that the Role is relevant to this work, participating, delegated, held by anyone, or required to be consulted;
- current-work capability relevance requires an explicit forward current-work declaration or typed Relation selecting that Role/capability for the bounded scope;
- semantic participation requires an explicit participant/relation declaration owned by qualified authority; Role presence, Handoff endpoint status, cache membership, transport placement and chat position are insufficient.

A fresh recipient must therefore distinguish **Role capability semantics** from **Role relevance**. `May Do` answers what a selected Role is capable/authorized to do in its declared Role boundary; it does not select that Role for current work.

### Participant, capability and source claim states

For participant/process/source grounding, Tooling must preserve at least these semantic outcomes without collapsing them:

- **established/qualified** — an exact semantic owner states the claim, an exact forward current-work selector makes it relevant, exact material resolves/qualifies, the scope matches, and no controlling contradiction blocks it;
- **not-established/unresolved** — the current claim is needed or inspected but its semantic owner/selector/material/scope is absent, unavailable, ambiguous, or only suggested by cache/inventory adjacency;
- **explicitly prohibited/blocked** — exact qualified authority denies the action/claim for the bounded scope or conflicts with another controlling boundary; preserve the contradiction/prohibition rather than degrading it to mere absence;
- **unknown/out-of-representation** — material or sibling context was not selected/carried and no semantic claim about its existence or nonexistence follows.

Absence from a cache is never an explicit prohibition. Presence in a cache is never an affirmative current-work grant.

Implementation-source authority follows the accepted holder/source Decision unchanged. Cached source/Workspace facts may identify exact source material; they do not authorize existing-source mutation or new-source creation. A cached Decision/Handoff/Relation may establish source authority only when that exact artifact owns the grant/prohibition and is forward-selected by current work. Host writability remains mechanical capability only.

### Minimum qualified closure for a blank/minimal recipient

A fresh recipient does not need a complete repository. The minimum truthful closure is the smallest forward-selected set sufficient to qualify the bounded action:

1. **Selected route authority** — exact selected Handoff identity/provenance, endpoints/capacities, Transfer, boundaries, Required/Reference Context declarations and completion expectation.
2. **Current frontier authority** — the exact current Task/Epic/Decision/Role declaration that bounds the work, or an exact pointer to it, with enough Parent/root continuity/integrity proof to qualify the selected lineage without loading unrelated ancestor bodies.
3. **Recipient Role closure** — exact recipient Role material plus recipient compatibility; explicit session binding assertion; exact binding authorization provenance; durable holder identity state kept separate.
4. **Selected material closure** — for each required/forward-selected target, its exact artifact/source coordinate, material class, digest/integrity basis when carried, coverage/provenance, availability and selector provenance. The closure may use complete Workspace bytes, bounded cache entries or exact references interchangeably when they qualify the same exact semantic target.
5. **Forward semantic claim closure** — for every participant/capability, process or implementation-source claim needed by the bounded work, the exact semantic owner plus its current-work selector path and scope/effect. If an adoption Decision and Relation are required, both are part of the closure; if one direct current-work declaration suffices, no extra Relation is required.
6. **Exact unresolved/blocker closure** — when a required claim/material cannot be qualified, name the missing semantic owner/material, the state that remains unresolved, the authority expected to supply it, and why the stronger action is blocked. Do not automatically expand discovery scope.

Everything else is optional/unknown. No rule requires carrying every Role, process, Relation or sibling source. Sparse work remains valid when the selected closure is sufficient.

### Tooling projection contract for Loom

Loom may implement equivalent field names and transport structures, but the projection must preserve the following semantics mechanically and without prose heuristics:

1. **Material qualification is not semantic classification.** Record exact cache/Workspace/pointer material identity, digest/provenance/coverage and availability independently from semantic claims.
2. **Artifact-owned facts retain provenance.** When Tooling exposes Role, Decision, Relation, process, Handoff or Workspace semantics from cached material, attribute each fact to the exact qualified artifact/schema/section or relation declaration, never to `cache` as authority.
3. **Every current-work claim requires a forward selector.** Positive participant/capability relevance, process applicability/requiredness and source authority must carry provenance to the exact current Handoff/Task/Role/Decision selector that made the semantic owner relevant. Core must not scan cache/workspace inventory or reverse Relations to manufacture that selector.
4. **Recipient Role mechanics stay distinct.** Project recipient compatibility, exact Role material, session binding assertion, binding authorization and durable holder identity separately. Recipient Role grounding pointers are discovery/material selectors only; they do not create semantic participation.
5. **Process dimensions stay distinct.** Project availability, applicability, requiredness, active execution, ownership and completion independently. Required Context membership is material requiredness, not process requiredness.
6. **Participant/capability projection stays sparse.** Project a Role/capability as current-work relevant only from explicit qualified semantic authority. If only exact Role material is cached, expose Role facts plus `relevance: not-established`; do not add it to a semantic participant map.
7. **Source authority stays independent from source material.** Project exact source identity/coverage and host writability separately from existing-source mutation authority and new-source creation authority.
8. **States fail visible.** Preserve established, unresolved/not-established, explicitly prohibited/blocked and out-of-representation/unknown distinctly enough that a recipient cannot mistake missing cache material for a prohibition or cached material for a grant.
9. **Every projected claim explains its basis.** Include semantic owner provenance, bounded scope/effect, current-work selector provenance, and material-resolution provenance sufficient for a cold recipient to understand why the claim is established or unresolved.
10. **No plan generation.** The grounding receipt may expose exact frontier, selected capabilities/process/source facts, blockers and boundaries, but it must not choose delegation, expand scope, create a work plan, infer missing participants, or turn orchestration diagnostics into a new lifecycle state.

An implementation may compact these fields, but it must not erase the separations above. If Core cannot project a claim without heuristic interpretation of arbitrary prose, it must leave that claim unresolved and expose the exact missing explicit projection/selector rather than inventing semantics.

### Mechanically accepted positive and negative cases

Accepted:

- a blank selected Workspace plus cache contains the exact Axiom Role; the Handoff selects `Axiom`, the Role says explicit-session assignment is permitted, and the session asserts `Axiom`: recipient compatibility and bounded session authorization may be established without durable Party identity;
- a Handoff directly requires process `P` for the transfer and Required Context points to exact cached `P`: applicability/requiredness comes from the Handoff, while process procedure bytes come from the cache;
- a current Task points forward to an adoption Decision + applicability Relation whose exact bytes are in cache: the chain can establish applicability when scope/effect qualify;
- current work explicitly selects Role `R` as reviewer/delegate/capability and the exact cached Role qualifies: Role relevance/capability may be established without treating other cached Roles as relevant;
- an exact forward-selected authority grants mutation of existing source `S`, while `S` is supplied as bounded cached source material: the grant and the source bytes remain distinct but jointly sufficient.

Rejected:

- Role `R` is in the cache, therefore `R` is a participant or relevant capability;
- process `P` and a Relation mentioning the Task are cached, therefore reverse-scan and apply `P`;
- Required Context contains `P`, therefore `P` is mandatory;
- a Role cache proves who currently holds the Role;
- a cached/writable Workspace authorizes implementation source creation;
- omitted cache siblings are treated as nonexistent or non-applicable;
- a recipient Handoff endpoint is normalized into the semantic participant map merely because the Role is active for transfer;
- Tooling searches remote repositories/connectors or adjacent files to resolve a missing semantic owner without explicit recovery authority.

### No missing semantic primitive

No missing canonical semantic primitive is demonstrated by this task. Existing Role, Handoff, Task/Epic, Decision, typed Relation, Workspace representation and exact reference/pointer semantics can express every required claim and boundary.

The remaining gap, if present after Loom implementation, is **projection/ergonomics**: Core may need a compact, explicit way to expose the forward selector chain and exact cache-material resolution provenance already authorized by existing semantics. That is not authority to invent a Cache schema, universal participant predicate, process-applicability predicate, holder identity object or global capability registry. A new semantic primitive should be considered only if a future concrete case cannot state the necessary claim truthfully with the existing controlling artifacts and typed Relations.

## Basis

- The accepted Thin-Lineage Anchor Grounding disposition already classifies complete Workspace, bounded/cache material, exact pointer/reference and unavailable source states and rejects Role inventory/package presence as participant authority.
- The accepted Process Applicability And Cold-Grounding disposition already requires target-side forward discoverability and separates availability, applicability, requiredness, execution, ownership and completion.
- The accepted Holder Binding And Implementation Source Authority disposition already separates recipient compatibility, session assertion, binding authorization, durable holder identity, source identity, existing-source mutation authority and new-source creation authority.
- `tiinex.handoff.v1` already gives Required Context exact material/purpose/availability/reference semantics while explicitly denying that context membership transfers responsibility or fabricates unavailable material.
- `tiinex.party.role.v1` already owns Role identity, authority/responsibility boundary and Holder Relationship without treating a Role artifact as proof of its holder.
- `tiinex.relation.v1` already preserves precise typed non-Parent edges with direction/scope/target and does not require a global predicate or Relation artifact for every edge.
- Workspace/source representation semantics already distinguish source identity and coverage from transport/package placement authority.
- The controlling Business Epic explicitly requires fresh thin-lineage recovery without treating carried Role inventory or repository presence as authority and keeps Business integration with Anchor and shared mechanics with Loom.
- Current Tooling grounding behavior already demonstrates the intended fail-visible shape: recipient Role/session binding can qualify while participant map, process applicability and implementation-source authority remain independently unresolved when no explicit semantic selector is present. That behavior is implementation evidence, not the source of this Decision.

## Consequences

- Anchor can reconcile this semantic return with Loom's parallel mechanical return without waiting for a new schema family.
- Blank/minimal Workspace acceptance may use exact route-bounded cache/Role material as a first-class **material source** so long as each stronger claim traces through an explicit semantic owner and forward selector.
- A fresh Role may be `grounded-to-act` for the bounded Handoff while participant map/process applicability/source authority dimensions remain unresolved when the bounded action does not require them; wider orchestration sufficiency stays a separate diagnostic.
- Blind validation should include positive cached-process and cached-relevant-Role cases plus negative nearby-cache cases proving that unselected Role/process/Relation material remains non-authoritative.
- No Business or Core mutation is performed by Axiom. Anchor owns Business integration and Loom owns Tooling implementation/reconciliation.

## Review Conditions

Review this Decision only if blind validation shows that a correctly authored forward selector cannot mechanically resolve exact cached authority without repository/reverse discovery; if a future canonical schema supersedes these composable semantics; or if Loom returns a concrete case in which no existing Role/Handoff/Task/Decision/Relation/Workspace declaration can truthfully own the required claim.

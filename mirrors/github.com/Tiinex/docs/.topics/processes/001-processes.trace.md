# Docs Processes

## Current Read

This topic is the local process root for work whose primary semantic authority or maintenance boundary belongs to the Tiinex Docs Workspace. It is organizationally descended from Tiinex's shared Processes topic in the Business Workspace without relocating Docs-local process authority into Business.

A local process may refer to qualified Roles, Handoffs, Evidence, Decisions, Tooling, and material from other Workspaces when the work requires them. Placement under this topic does not create Role-holder state, transfer responsibility, or make Docs the owner of those referenced domains.

## Design Direction

Keep Workspace-local processes close to the authority they govern while preserving organizational ancestry through real Parent continuity. Do not create pointer-only artifacts merely to connect local process catalogs back to Tiinex organizational process ancestry.

The first local process under this topic is Tiinex Schema Development, because schema source authority and schema-development maintenance are Docs-local even when Core Tooling and Business Roles participate in execution.

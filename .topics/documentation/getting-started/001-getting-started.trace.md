# Getting Started

**The easiest way to begin is to describe your idea in the chat.** You do not need to learn commands, file formats, or Tiinex vocabulary first.

For example, you might say: *“I want to make a small board game. Can you help me get started?”* Your assistant should help with the game itself. When it becomes something you want to continue another day, Tiinex can help keep its files and important decisions together.

## Your first project, in plain language

1. **Tell the assistant your goal.** You can begin with a rough idea. It is fine if the first answer is a helpful question or a sketch, rather than a folder of records.
2. **Share your project material when there is some.** If you already have a Tiinex Workspace package, attach it to the chat and let the assistant follow its supplied starting instructions. If you do not have one, ask how to prepare a place to keep your work; the available steps depend on your chat and connected tools. [Your first Workspace](001-1-your-first-workspace.trace.md).
3. **Make progress before adding structure.** Let the assistant help create the game, document, program, or plan. It may suggest keeping an ongoing project or a small record of the next action, but you do not have to select artifact types yourself. [From a goal to lasting work](001-2-from-goal-to-qualified-work.trace.md).
4. **Use the support that is actually available.** An assistant with qualified Tiinex access can discover relevant records and check how they must be written. You need not run commands or memorize a list. [Discover and use Tiinex](001-3-discover-and-use-tiinex.trace.md).
5. **Save what matters when you want to return.** Ask: *“Can we keep this so I can pick it up another day?”* Confirm what is preserved and how to bring it into a new conversation. [Preserve and continue](001-4-preserve-and-continue.trace.md).

You can always stop after step 1. Tiinex should fit your project, not make your project fit a checklist.

## Words you might encounter

- **Workspace:** a place for project files and records of important work. It can be local and need not be a Git repository.
- **Record (artifact):** a readable account of a goal, task, decision, observation, or other piece of work.
- **Schema:** rules for a particular kind of record. The assistant can look these up when needed.
- **Handoff:** a way to carry enough qualified context to another session or participant.

## What a useful first session looks like

You receive help with your actual goal, understand what has been kept, and know what to do when you come back. A chat reply alone is not a guaranteed save. A Workspace package is not evidence that the game or plan itself is correct. If your assistant cannot store or package files in the current host, it should say so, not pretend it did.

**For assistants:** Do not assume that a supplied Workspace automatically owns the new idea, or that an empty one is irrelevant. Choose the smallest qualified structure appropriate to the user's intent, and explain the benefit in everyday words. Do not auto-deploy a project template.

Learn more when you need to: [Guides](../guides/001-guides.trace.md).

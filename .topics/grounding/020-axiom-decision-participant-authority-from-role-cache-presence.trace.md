# Semantic Conclusion

The proposition is affirmed:

> Package/cache presence of a qualified Role, by itself, must not establish semantic participant membership for current work.

A qualified Role can be carried for grounding, dependency resolution, or endpoint qualification without thereby becoming a semantic participant in the current work. Participant membership is a separate authority claim.

## Qualified Basis

1. The qualified grounding projection classifies carried Role and lineage-cache material as diagnostic carriage evidence only and states that presence does not create semantic authority beyond the requirement or material it exactly resolves.
2. The current Axiom holder binding is qualified from exact selected-Handoff consumption plus Axiom Role assignment-mode authority (`handoff`). That binding authorizes the bounded holder assertion mechanism, but its own authority boundary explicitly does not establish participation, durable identity, delegation, process, source, or acceptance authority.
3. Current grounding reports `participant-map-not-established`: no explicit semantic participant declaration or authoritative participant Relation is present in the current grounding input, and carried Role pointers remain grounding-only.
4. The same qualified grounding identifies the authority needed for a positive participant claim: an explicit qualified participant declaration/projection from semantic authority, including an authoritative participant Relation where applicable. Role/cache inventory, Handoff endpoint labels, filenames, transport position, or chat position are insufficient substitutes.

## Semantic Distinction

- **Role/cache/grounding presence** establishes that qualified material is available to resolve a bounded grounding requirement.
- **Holder binding** establishes only the authorized Role assertion for this consuming session or Handoff when the Role's exact assignment modes permit it.
- **Semantic participant membership** is an independent current-work claim and requires explicit qualified participant authority.

These claims must remain distinct even when the same Role artifact participates in more than one of them.

## Boundary

This Decision is limited to the exact proposition delegated by the controlling Task. It does not create a participant map, select a delegate, alter Business, grant publication authority, or broaden source/process authority. It records the Axiom-owned semantic conclusion for return to Anchor.

## Return Result

Result: **affirmed**. A fresh consumer must not infer current participant membership merely because a qualified Role is present in package/cache material. Any positive participant claim must be grounded in explicit qualified semantic participant authority.

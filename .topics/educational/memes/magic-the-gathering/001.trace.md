# Magic: The Gathering Memes

This topic narrows the memes root to Magic: The Gathering-framed meme concepts.

## Current Read

Artifacts here should assume the main signal is Magic: The Gathering recognition: cards, stack logic, rules text, legendary framing, mana-color identity, or collectible-game rhetoric applied to continuity, trace, proof, inheritance, or transfer.

## Design Direction

- keep the read anchored in Magic-like card logic, rules interpretation, or flavor-text framing rather than in generic fantasy art alone
- use the recognition surface to make lineage feel playable, rule-bounded, and easy to remember rather than merely ornamental
- prefer concrete continuity hooks such as stack resolution, triggered abilities, graveyard recovery, legendary inheritance, tutoring, copies, counters, or persistent enchantment-like lineage
- avoid drifting into broad fantasy-reference humor if the continuity or lineage signal is no longer legible

## Desired Transfer Signal

- the joke should make lineage feel like something the public can learn through a recognizable rules system, not only something infra people already understand
- the framing should preserve enough rule clarity that later meme variants can attach different mechanics without losing the shared lineage point
- later public-facing image or outreach artifacts should be able to attach cleanly to this leaf without first rebuilding the Magic recognition frame from scratch

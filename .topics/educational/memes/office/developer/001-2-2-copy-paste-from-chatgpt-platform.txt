Git kopplar ihop versioner.

Det är lite som:

Internet:
Where is the resource?

Web:
What links to what?

Tiinex:
What continues what, why, from where, under which schema, with which policy?

Decentralized internet with receipts.

Eller ännu bättre:

Decentralized provenance for human/AI context.

“Kan data flytta och fortfarande bära ursprungets vilja?”

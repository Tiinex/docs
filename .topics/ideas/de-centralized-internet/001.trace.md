# Decentralized Context Layer

This topic keeps the broad "de-centralized internet" phrase anchored to the narrower claim that actually seems useful here: a portable provenance/context layer.

## Current Read

The useful claim is not to rebuild the Internet.

The useful claim is to make separate lineages, tools, and media able to find one another, verify one another, and continue one another without surrendering their receipts to a single cloud surface.

That is closer to decentralized provenance for human/AI context than to a general internet rewrite.

The comparison that seems to matter here is:

- Internet: network of networks
- Web: links between documents
- Git: links between versions
- Tiinex: links between why something exists, where it came from, what it builds on, and how it gets continued

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- receipts and lineage over anonymous transport
- portable context over account lock-in
- local-first control with optional federation
- explicit origin, parent, and continuation surfaces
- interoperability between separate lineages rather than one monolithic platform

## Good Child Candidates

- decentralized provenance for human/AI context
- portable receipts and verification
- federation and interoperability
- local-first social/context clients
- policy-aware exchange between lineages

## Next Artifacts

- continue a narrower child when one concrete receiver or product boundary becomes clear
- split off interoperability or trust surfaces if they become distinct enough
- attach implementation grounding later when the line needs more than concept framing

# Portable Lineage Devices

This topic narrows the hardware branch into a concrete product-family direction:
physical devices that help lineage survive outside one cloud vendor, one active
session, or one live runtime.

## Current Read

The attached concept artifacts strongly suggest a coherent device family rather
than one isolated gadget.

The family read is:

- capture context in the field
- preserve it in human-readable and machine-usable form
- move it across devices or environments
- rebuild or verify continuity later
- keep recovery possible even under partial failure, offline conditions, or bad
  sorting

## Artifact Grounding Read

The current grounding from the attached artifacts points especially toward these
device concepts:

- a matrix printer plus scanner for printing and re-ingesting lineage
- a pocket trace recorder for field capture
- a lineage dock that merges fragments from multiple carriers
- a field evidence camera that stamps provenance into capture flow
- an offline sync key for portable continuity transfer
- an archive crate scanner for bulk recovery from old physical material

These are still concept-level signals, not product-ready specifications.

## Design Direction

This line should continue as a hardware family topic, not as a single-device
pitch.

Good continuation should usually prefer:

- preserving the shared device-family logic here
- opening one child per device concept only when the receiver, workflow, or
  build constraints materially diverge
- keeping portability, recoverability, and verification as the family-level
  invariants
- leaving manufacturing, BOM, and electronics specifics to later children

## Shared Device Invariants

Across this family, strong downstream ideas should usually preserve:

- offline tolerance
- durable human readability where useful
- machine-actionable structure where useful
- checksum or verification-friendly continuity
- portable import and export between carriers
- recoverability after disorder, interruption, or partial loss

## Good Child Candidates

- `matrix-printer-scanner`
- `pocket-trace-recorder`
- `lineage-dock`
- `field-evidence-camera`
- `offline-sync-key`
- `archive-crate-scanner`

## Next Artifacts

- continue one child per device when the workflow and receiver are clear enough
  to justify their own lineage
- attach software companions later where a device depends on a paired app,
  ingest flow, or graph reconstruction surface

# Public Provenance Viewer

This topic narrows the recoverable-context app family into a reader for public
or external audiences who need trustworthy proof without gaining mutation
authority.

## Current Read

The strongest value here is receiver clarity.

An outside reader should be able to understand enough of the lineage, evidence,
and trust surface to evaluate a claim without opening raw internal tooling or
needing operator folklore.

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- read-only trust surfaces over internal complexity leakage
- clear distinction between verified, linked, and missing material
- audience legibility over maximal graph density
- leaving auth, hosting, and publishing mechanics to later children

## Likely Receiver Humans

Strong downstream artifacts here should usually stay useful for:

- customers or prospects evaluating claims
- auditors or reviewers
- public readers needing proof context
- partners or collaborators consuming shared evidence

## Good Child Candidates

- public trust summary model
- verified versus unverified material cues
- evidence browsing and source reopening
- redaction and share-safe views
- export and embed surfaces

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one public-proof workflow becomes concrete
  enough to deserve deeper design work
- attach provenance and site-surface grounding later when this line needs
  stronger implementation proof

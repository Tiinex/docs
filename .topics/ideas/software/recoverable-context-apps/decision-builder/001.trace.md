# Decision Builder

This topic narrows the recoverable-context app family into a decision-focused
tool that makes reasoning durable enough to review, reopen, or hand off later.

## Current Read

The strongest value here is not just writing down a decision.

The stronger value is preserving:

- the options that were considered
- what evidence mattered
- what was chosen
- why the choice was acceptable at the time
- what later readers need in order to revisit or verify it

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- bounded decision workflows over giant generic note-taking
- explicit linkage between reasoning and evidence
- support for reopenable decisions instead of fake finality
- leaving exact template, UI, and automation behavior to later children

## Likely Receiver Humans

Strong downstream artifacts here should usually stay useful for:

- project leads
- operational decision makers
- teams making auditable internal choices
- humans handing off decisions across shifts or roles

## Good Child Candidates

- option and tradeoff capture
- evidence linking and confidence levels
- decision approval and authority surfaces
- reopen and revision workflows
- export to lineage and public proof surfaces

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one decision workflow slice becomes concrete
  enough for deeper design work
- attach companion business or provenance grounding later when this line needs
  stronger operational proof

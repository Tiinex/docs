# DIY Ideas Branch

This branch holds ideas whose likely human-in-the-loop receivers are hands-on
builders rather than only buyers, operators, or software teams.

That includes hobbyists, makers, repair-minded users, homelab operators,
workshop tinkerers, and technically capable people who prefer to build, modify,
host, repair, or adapt things themselves.

## Current Read

The point of this branch is not just consumer hardware or side projects.

The point is to preserve a lane for ideas that become real through practical
building, improvisation, iteration, and direct handling of tools, components,
devices, and local systems.

## Design Direction

For this branch, continuation should usually prefer:

- practical buildability over abstract product theater
- hackable systems over sealed black-box assumptions
- repair, reuse, local control, and understandable tooling as strong positives
- keeping the branch broad enough to support workshop, homelab, repair, and
  build-tool lines without collapsing them into one blob

## Human-In-The-Loop Read

Good child topics under this branch should usually help later readers answer:

- what a competent DIY person could realistically build, adapt, or host
- what skills, tools, or materials the line assumes
- whether the idea is best treated as workshop build, repair path, homelab
  system, or reusable maker tool
- what later evidence or task artifacts would be needed before a build becomes
  seriously reproducible

## Current Child Candidates

## Next Artifacts

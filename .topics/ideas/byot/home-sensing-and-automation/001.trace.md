# Home Sensing And Automation

This topic narrows the DIY branch into a line for builders who want homes,
garages, sheds, workshops, or small properties to be more observable and more
controllable through systems they can understand and adapt.

## Current Read

The strongest value here is not generic smart-home convenience.

The stronger value is local sensing, local control, and automations that remain
inspectable enough to troubleshoot, improve, and trust.

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- local control over cloud-only dependence
- understandable sensing and triggers over opaque magic routines
- practical household, workshop, or property value over gadget clutter
- leaving exact protocols, sensors, and hub choices to later children

## Good Child Candidates

- environment sensing and logging
- local safety and alert systems
- power and energy awareness
- workshop and storage monitoring
- home dashboards and control surfaces

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one sensing or automation slice becomes clear
  enough to deserve its own lineage
- attach hardware, software, or hosting companions later when a child needs
  stronger implementation grounding

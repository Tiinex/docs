# Repair And Refurbish

This topic narrows the DIY branch into a line for people who repair, restore,
upgrade, repurpose, or salvage devices and systems instead of treating them as
sealed throwaway products.

## Current Read

The strongest value here is not nostalgia.

The stronger value is practical reuse: extending useful life, recovering value,
avoiding needless dependence, and making systems understandable enough to keep
working.

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- repairable and inspectable systems over sealed replacements
- practical upgrade and salvage paths over idealized green messaging
- reusable methods that a competent builder can repeat with normal tools
- leaving exact part sourcing, board-level detail, and model-specific quirks to
  later children

## Good Child Candidates

- device upgrades and part swaps
- storage and laptop refurb paths
- salvageable home infrastructure
- repair documentation and checklists
- reuse of retired enterprise gear in DIY settings

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one repair family or refurb workflow becomes
  concrete enough to deserve its own lineage
- attach evidence or task artifacts later when a child needs more exact
  reproducibility

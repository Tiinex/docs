# Sales Ideas Branch

This branch is for ideas whose main receiving humans are likely to be sales
operators, founders handling revenue conversations, account owners, or teams
responsible for commercial motion.

## Current Read

Strong ideas in this lane should usually help a human sales process become:

- clearer
- more trustworthy
- easier to inspect
- easier to hand off
- better aligned with how real prospects evaluate claims

## Design Direction

For this branch, continuation should usually prefer:

- improving real sales motion rather than inventing vague growth language
- making proof, trust, qualification, and follow-up more concrete
- preserving room for both outbound and inbound sales-adjacent ideas
- deferring detailed scripts, tooling choices, and channel tactics to later
  children

## Good Child Shapes

Likely useful child shapes include:

- qualification and triage
- sales evidence and proof surfaces
- CRM workflow support
- follow-up and handoff quality
- buyer education during complex decisions

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower topics when one concrete sales pain or trust problem merits
  its own lineage
- add evidence-backed children later when a subtopic needs grounded commercial
  examples or workflow material

# Buyer Proof Surfaces

This topic narrows proof-first positioning into a practical sales question:
what concrete proof surfaces should a buyer be able to inspect before they take
the offer seriously.

## Current Read

The strongest commercial signal here is that some buyers will not be persuaded
by slogans about AI, cloud, or automation.

They will move when they can inspect a proof surface that makes the claim more
than rhetorical.

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- inspectable demos over glossy messaging alone
- evidence and verification affordances over abstract differentiation language
- clear buyer readability over operator-centric jargon
- leaving exact packaging of the sales process to later children

## Likely Receiver Humans

Strong downstream artifacts here should usually stay useful for:

- technical buyers
- skeptical founders
- operational evaluators
- procurement-adjacent reviewers
- partners checking whether the claims are real

## Good Child Candidates

- live demo proof surfaces
- read-only trust views for prospects
- evidence-backed comparison materials
- verification checklists for evaluation
- post-demo follow-up artifacts

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one buyer-facing proof surface becomes clear
  enough to justify its own lineage
- attach public viewer, trace explorer, or demo-grounding later when this line
  needs stronger proof material

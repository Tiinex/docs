# Managed Backup And Recovery

This topic narrows the business-IT branch into a line for teams that provide,
operate, or depend on backup and recovery as an actual managed capability.

## Current Read

The strongest value here is not merely keeping copies.

The stronger value is making recovery real, testable, and operationally owned.

That means backup policy, restore speed, verification, customer communication,
and recovery confidence all matter more than raw storage volume alone.

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- restore confidence over backup theater
- service ownership and recovery workflow over raw storage language
- explicit proof of recoverability over assumption-based compliance
- leaving exact storage engine, appliance, and tooling choices to later
  children

## Likely Receiver Humans

Strong downstream artifacts here should usually stay useful for:

- backup operators
- managed service providers
- infrastructure and continuity owners
- IT managers responsible for recovery readiness
- teams supporting customers through restore and incident events

## Good Child Candidates

- backup verification and restore drills
- multi-tenant managed backup services
- customer recovery workflows and communication
- long-term archive versus hot recovery design
- proof surfaces for recovery readiness

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one recovery or service slice becomes concrete
  enough to deserve its own lineage
- attach storage, hosting, or evidence grounding later when a child needs
  stronger implementation proof

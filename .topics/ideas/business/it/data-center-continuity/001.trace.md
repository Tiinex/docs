# Data Center Continuity

This topic narrows the business-IT branch into the continuity discipline around
data center and facility-backed infrastructure.

## Current Read

The key idea here is not just running servers in a building.

The stronger idea is making continuity visible and manageable across outages,
migrations, recovery events, partial failures, and maintenance windows.

This gives the line strong overlap with Tiinex-style proof, lineage, and
recoverability values.

## Design Direction

Good continuation in this line should usually prefer:

- operational continuity over raw infrastructure inventory
- disaster recovery and failover clarity over abstract architecture diagrams
- explicit recovery procedures, evidence surfaces, and ownership boundaries
- leaving site-specific topology and vendor selection to later children

## Likely Receiver Humans

Strong downstream artifacts here should usually stay useful for:

- data center operations leads
- platform reliability owners
- infrastructure managers
- continuity and disaster recovery planners
- managed hosting teams with facility responsibility

## Good Child Candidates

- recovery drills and proof surfaces
- migration and cutover continuity
- multi-site resilience
- backup verification workflows
- outage reconstruction and operator handoff

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower children when one resilience or recovery slice becomes clear
  enough to stand on its own
- attach evidence or tool-grounding later when a child needs stronger runtime
  or operational proof

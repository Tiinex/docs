# Business IT Ideas Branch

This branch is for ideas where the human-in-the-loop receivers are likely to be
internal IT owners, system administrators, workflow owners, integration leads,
or technology-minded business operators.

## Current Read

This branch should hold ideas that improve how an organization runs, connects,
governs, or observes internal systems.

The key distinction is that these ideas are still business-facing even when
technology is involved.

They should not collapse into pure developer-tool ideas unless the intended
receivers are actually engineering teams rather than business IT owners.

## Design Direction

For this branch, continuation should usually prefer:

- internal workflow improvement over outward product speculation
- operational clarity over flashy automation claims
- ideas that make ownership, integration, compliance, support, or visibility
  easier for real organizations
- leaving hard implementation choices to later children once the problem and
  receiving function are clear

## Good Child Shapes

Likely useful child shapes include:

- integration and synchronization
- IT service workflows
- procurement and asset visibility
- governance and auditability
- internal knowledge or process support

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower topics when one concrete internal pain or opportunity is
  strong enough to deserve its own line
- attach evidence or repo-grounding later if a child starts depending on real
  workflow tooling, provenance, or system examples

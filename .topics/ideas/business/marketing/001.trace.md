# Marketing Ideas Branch

This branch is for ideas whose main receiving humans are likely to be marketers,
audience builders, founders shaping public narrative, content operators, or
teams trying to earn attention and understanding.

## Current Read

Strong ideas in this lane should usually help a public message become:

- clearer
- more memorable
- easier to distribute
- better matched to the right audience
- more likely to produce informed attention instead of empty reach

## Design Direction

For this branch, continuation should usually prefer:

- audience understanding over empty traffic metrics
- clearer positioning and narrative over generic brand language
- ideas that improve how signal is packaged, distributed, or interpreted
- deferring exact campaign structure, channel mix, and content tooling to later
  children

## Good Child Shapes

Likely useful child shapes include:

- positioning and category framing
- audience education and narrative arcs
- content systems and distribution support
- public proof and trust-building surfaces
- meme, visual, or message amplification paths

## Next Artifacts

- continue narrower topics when one concrete marketing pain or audience problem
  merits its own lineage
- add evidence-backed children later when a subtopic needs grounded channel,
  content, or public-surface examples

# Business Ideas Branch

This branch holds ideas whose primary continuation surface is business-facing.

That includes ideas where the next serious reader is more likely to be a
manager, operator, buyer, process owner, or business function lead than a pure
technical builder.

## Current Read

The important role of this branch is not only domain labeling.

Its role is to keep business-oriented ideas coherent enough that later LLM work
can expand them in a way that still fits a real human receiver lane.

## Design Direction

For this branch, continuation should usually prefer:

- organizing children by receiving business function when that improves handoff
- keeping the branch broad enough to support many business idea families
- reserving implementation detail and detailed validation plans for narrower
  child topics
- making value proposition, workflow impact, and human ownership visible early

## Human-In-The-Loop Read

Good child topics under this branch should usually help a later reviewer answer:

- who inside a business would realistically own the idea
- what operating pain, revenue opportunity, or coordination problem the idea
  addresses
- what kind of proof or pilot would be needed before serious execution

## Current Child Candidates

- `finance`
- `it`
- `management`
- `marketing`
- `sales`

Future siblings may still be added when a real receiver lane appears, such as
operations, finance, HR, or customer support.

## Next Artifacts

- continue `finance` as the capital, cashflow, risk, and control branch
- continue `it` as the internal systems and workflow branch
- continue `management` as the coordination, steering, and execution branch
- continue `marketing` as the attention, positioning, and audience-growth branch
- continue `sales` as the revenue and customer-motion branch
- add later siblings only when their receiver lane is materially different from
  the existing children

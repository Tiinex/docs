# Composable Operational Grounding Disposition

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: Foundation composable operational grounding
- Decision: accept Axiom's bounded semantic disposition. Reusable operational grounding remains composed from the exact guidance artifact under its true semantic owner, an explicit Decision when that guidance becomes operative, an explicit typed Relation when bounded applicability must be carried beyond the selected Handoff's own scope, and Handoff Required Context when the exact material is necessary for a cold work turn. Do not introduce a generic Policy/Operating Guidance schema, Role inheritance model, privileged policy pointer channel, or package-derived applicability rule for this seam.

## Basis

- Axiom's completed discovery found maintained Party/Role, Decision, Relation, Handoff, and context-owner semantics sufficient while preserving fail-closed applicability.
- The current package path already proves that exact Required Context can be cold-started from carried bytes without inventing semantic applicability from package placement.
- Foundation needs recoverable operational behavior before broader acceleration, but adding a generic policy family would currently duplicate existing owners and create a new normalization problem rather than close one.

## Consequences

- Business will dogfood the model by explicitly adopting its current Foundation cross-repository work-turn representation through Decision and bounded applicability Relation rather than treating proposed Topic prose as silently governing.
- No Loom implementation is required merely to normalize policies: current Tooling should preserve exact Handoff Required Context and typed Relation material without promoting free-text predicates into privileged machine semantics.
- Fresh recipients must fail closed when operative adoption or applicability is required but the exact Decision, target, or typed Relation cannot be recovered.
- Role prose remains authoritative only for the Role's own bounded capacity and collaboration boundary; reusable cross-context behavior must not be smuggled in through Role inheritance.
- A dedicated Process schema remains a separate future question about stable process lifecycle/body semantics, not a prerequisite for this grounding closure.

## Review Conditions

- Review if repeated operational guidance instances require deterministic machine normalization that cannot be expressed without guessing from Relation labels.
- Review if stable process artifacts need lifecycle/body semantics that Topic plus Decision cannot preserve cleanly.
- Review if cold recipients still require hidden chat/runtime state after exact Role, Handoff, Decision, Relation, process/guidance, and target context are carried.

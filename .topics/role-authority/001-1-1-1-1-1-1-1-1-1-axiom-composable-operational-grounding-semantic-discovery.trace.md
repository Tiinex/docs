# Composable Operational Grounding Semantic Boundary Discovery

## Discovery Intent

- Intent: determine whether reusable operational guidance and bounded applicability are already expressible through maintained Tiinex semantics strongly enough for a fresh recipient to recover how to work without duplicated Role prose or hidden chat/runtime memory.
- Starting Question: can current Party/Role, Decision/process-adjacent, Relation, Handoff, Event Session, Project, Annotation, Interaction Unit, and other maintained semantics compose one truthful operational-grounding model, or is a new generic Policy/Operating Guidance schema irreducibly required?

## Discovery Field

- Field: the exact carried Business, Docs, and Site Workspace snapshots qualified for the selected Axiom cold-start route; the selected Handoff and its exact Required Context; current maintained Docs schemas for Role, Relation, Handoff, Decision, Topic, Event Session, Project, Annotation, Semantic Annotation, and Interaction Unit; and the carried Business cross-repository process representation.
- In Scope: reusable guidance content ownership; operative adoption; applicability to Role, Handoff, Project, Session, or another bounded context; cold-recipient composition; fail-closed machine projection; distinction between human-readable semantic recovery and privileged machine normalization.
- Out Of Scope: Tooling implementation; remote repository mutation; a new Role inheritance model; holder assignment; delegation; Handoff acceptance; responsibility merge; host-safety behavior; package-placement semantics; runtime-private prompt authority; automatic promotion of process prose into universal policy.

## Discovery Method

- Method: perform the declared Tiinex bootstrap and preferred-path cold-start qualification; inspect the exact carried Required Context first; inspect the selected Handoff Reference Context and maintained schema families through the qualified Docs Workspace; compare each candidate family by its declared main semantic job; inspect the carried Business process representation as a real current example; prefer composition of existing owners; and fail closed where a specialized machine projection would require normalizing an undeclared predicate.
- Cold-Start Qualification: preferred path passed. The exact selected Handoff and all five Required Context items resolved to carried bytes before substantive work.
- Primary Existing Owners Examined: `tiinex.party.role.v1`, `tiinex.relation.v1`, `tiinex.handoff.v1`, `tiinex.decision.v1`, `tiinex.topic.v1`, `tiinex.event.session.v1`, `tiinex.project.v1`, `tiinex.annotation.v1`, `tiinex.semantic.annotation.v1`, and `tiinex.interaction.unit.v1`.
- Process Example: Business `.topics/processes/002-cross-repository-work-turn-process.trace.md` is currently a `tiinex.topic.v1` artifact whose body preserves a reusable operating-process representation while explicitly avoiding a claim that every project must use it.

## Discovery Boundaries

- Human-First Boundary: declared artifact meaning remains the primary shared semantic surface. LLM/runtime state may recover or explain that meaning but must not invent applicability, authority, adoption, or missing context.
- Role Boundary: Role owns a bounded capacity, authority/responsibility boundary, and holder relationship. Reusing guidance must not become Role inheritance, holder propagation, delegation, or responsibility merge.
- Decision Boundary: Decision owns a landed or explicitly stated operative choice and what it applies to. It may make exact guidance content operative, but it does not prove delegation authority, legal force, holder identity, recipient acceptance, or universal scope.
- Relation Boundary: Relation owns an explicit typed non-Parent relationship with readable type, direction, scope, target, uncertainty, and limits. One declared relation instance may truthfully express applicability, but generic Relation capacity does not create a globally normalized predicate or privileged policy channel.
- Handoff Boundary: Handoff Required Context can make exact guidance/adoption/applicability material necessary for one transfer's interpretation or execution. Required Context proves neither global applicability nor transfer of responsibility for the referenced material.
- Context-Owner Boundary: Event Session and Project own bounded activity/project context; Annotation owns bounded extra information on a target; Semantic Annotation owns labeling/classification; Interaction Unit owns implementation-neutral interaction semantics. None of these families should be widened into generic policy authority merely because they can carry instructions, labels, or references.
- Topic/Process Boundary: Topic can preserve a bounded working process thread and current operating-process representation. When that process is meant to become an operative governing choice, Decision should own the adoption/state rather than silently upgrading Topic prose into authority.
- Package Boundary: carriage, adjacency, package pointers, Workspace representation, Required Context availability, authorship, filename, path, runtime loading, and provider instructions do not themselves establish semantic applicability.

## Discovery Outcome

### 1. Disposition — reject a new generic Policy/Operating Guidance schema for this seam

Current maintained authority is sufficient for composable operational grounding. A new `tiinex.policy.v1` or generic Operating Guidance family is not required to solve the transferred problem and would currently risk collapsing distinct truths that already have explicit owners.

The smallest composition is:

1. **Guidance content stays with its true semantic owner.** A process-like working representation may remain a `tiinex.topic.v1` artifact while it is a bounded live/process thread. If the content is actually a Task, Instrument, Event Session, Interaction Unit, Annotation, or another existing semantic family, that family remains authoritative for its own meaning.
2. **Decision owns operative adoption.** When exact guidance is intended to govern work rather than merely describe, propose, explain, or label it, a `tiinex.decision.v1` artifact should state the landed decision, identify the exact guidance material, make the affected scope readable, and state the operative effect. This is the existing canonical owner for “what now governs.”
3. **Relation owns bounded applicability.** The guidance/adoption artifact may project an explicit typed non-Parent relation when another artifact owns the main semantics, using a precise relation declaration such as `Relation Type: operational guidance applies to`, `Relation Direction: current guidance/adoption -> target context`, and a bounded `Relation Scope`, with the exact Role, Handoff, Project, Event Session, or other target. A standalone Relation artifact may be used when the relation instance itself has independent provenance/lifecycle worth preserving. The exact readable predicate and target are authoritative for that instance only.
4. **Handoff owns cold-turn necessity.** When the selected work cannot be understood or executed without that guidance, the Handoff lists the exact operative Decision/guidance and any separately materialized applicability Relation in `Required Context`; otherwise they may remain `Reference Context`. This makes the material available and blocking for that Handoff without promoting package carriage into semantic applicability.
5. **The context artifact remains separately authoritative.** Role, Handoff, Project, Event Session, or another target continues to own its own identity, state, scope, responsibility, and interpretation limits. Applicability does not merge those semantics into the guidance artifact.

### 2. Applicability predicate and binding

The canonical semantic binding available today is an **explicit typed Relation declaration**, not a hidden package edge and not a new universal predicate registry.

For a concrete instance, a readable relation equivalent to the following is sufficient:

- Relation Type: operational guidance applies to
- Relation Direction: current guidance/adoption -> target context
- Relation Scope: bounded execution-context applicability
- Target: exact Role, Handoff, Project, Event Session, or other artifact/descriptor
- Interpretation Limits: this relation does not mean inheritance, holder assignment, delegation, acceptance, consent, responsibility transfer, or universal applicability

This relation instance is canonical for its declared target and scope because Relation already owns typed non-Parent relationships. It does **not** make `operational guidance applies to` a globally registered predicate, and Tooling must not normalize arbitrary labels into a privileged field without later explicit predicate/binding authority.

A Decision may also state what it applies to in its readable body because the Decision contract explicitly requires the affected subject/scope to be legible. That readable Decision scope and the typed Relation must not contradict one another. If both are present and materially conflict, applicability is ambiguous and must fail closed.

### 3. Fresh-recipient grounding composition

A cold recipient grounding one work turn may compose only exact declared material:

- the endpoint Role/capacity material declared by the selected Handoff;
- the selected Handoff, including Transfers, Required Context, Reference Context distinction, retained responsibilities, exclusions/dependencies, and completion expectation;
- the exact guidance content artifact under its existing semantic owner;
- the exact Decision that makes that guidance operative when governing status matters;
- the exact typed Relation declaration that binds the operative guidance to the relevant Role/Handoff/Project/Session/context when applicability is not already limited solely by the selected Handoff's own Required Context and readable Decision scope;
- the target context artifact itself when its boundary/state matters;
- required interaction/annotation material only when those artifacts genuinely own additional interaction or annotation semantics needed for execution.

These artifacts are composed for interpretation; they do not become one merged artifact or one inherited Role.

### 4. Fail-closed rules

A recipient or Tooling implementation must not infer operational applicability from:

- package inclusion, package path, carrier dimension, adjacency, filename, lineage numbering, pointer presence, Workspace membership, transport selection, or runtime loading;
- `Required Context` alone when no artifact meaning says the material governs/applies beyond the selected Handoff need;
- Role prose similarity, a shared Role label, a shared project name, authorship, current holder heuristics, or a prior chat/session instruction;
- an arbitrary Relation label normalized into inheritance, delegation, holder propagation, responsibility merge, participant status, or universal policy authority.

If operative adoption is required but no Decision or equivalent existing semantic owner establishes it, preserve the guidance's actual state (for example proposed, descriptive, or reference-only) rather than treating it as governing.

If applicability beyond one explicitly bounded Handoff cannot be recovered from an exact typed Relation and readable target/scope, applicability is unresolved. Do not search by naming convention or assume “all roles,” “all sessions,” or “this package.”

If a target is unresolved, keep it unresolved. If Decision scope and Relation scope conflict, surface ambiguity. If guidance content is stale/superseded, use the owning artifact's state/supersession semantics rather than silently continuing to apply it.

### 5. Why Annotation, Interaction Unit, Event Session, and Project do not close this alone

- Annotation can attach bounded extra information to a target, but its contract explicitly separates annotation value from target content and does not create governing authority by implication.
- Semantic Annotation is labeling/classification, not an operative instruction or authority relation.
- Interaction Unit may carry a human instruction and definition binding, but its main job is interaction semantics and implementation-neutral affordances, not cross-context operating authority.
- Event Session and Project can carry bounded activity/project state and references, but their contracts explicitly defer companion semantics to the appropriate owner and must not replace Decision or Relation when those own the main meaning.

These families may participate in grounding when their own semantics are required, but they should not be promoted into generic policy containers.

### 6. Process-schema note

The absence of a dedicated generic `Process` schema is not the same semantic gap as operational applicability. Current Business process representations can remain Topic artifacts while they are bounded working process threads, and a Decision can make an exact process artifact operative for an explicit scope.

If later product needs justify a maintained Process schema for stable process lifecycle/body validation independent of Decision and applicability, that should be evaluated as a separate schema-family question. It should not be introduced indirectly as `Policy` merely to solve recipient grounding.

### 7. Tooling-facing projection

No new privileged `policyPointers`, `operatingGuidancePointers`, Role inheritance channel, or package-derived applicability channel is semantically required by this result.

A conforming Tooling implementation should:

- preserve exact selected Handoff and Required Context closure before substantive work;
- preserve exact Decision/guidance artifacts and exact typed Relation declarations when they are carried as required material;
- expose Relation Type, Direction, Scope, target, uncertainty, and interpretation limits without inventing normalized semantics;
- keep Role authority, holder state, Handoff transfer, guidance content, operative Decision, applicability Relation, context state, and completion expectation as separate projections;
- treat `Required Context` as cold-turn necessity, not as universal applicability;
- leave any convenience `applicableGuidance` projection unavailable unless a later canonical predicate/binding explicitly authorizes deterministic normalization;
- never recover applicability from discovery-only package Role pointers, filenames, paths, provider prompts, conversation memory, or runtime-private state.

No Site Tooling implementation is authorized by this Discovery. Anchor may route Loom only after accepting this semantic disposition.

### 8. Smallest authority-change set

- No change required: Party/Role.
- No change required: Relation.
- No change required: Handoff.
- No change required: Decision.
- No change required: Event Session or Project.
- No change required: Annotation or Interaction Unit families.
- No new generic Policy/Operating Guidance schema.
- No privileged machine applicability predicate/channel is required for the current Handoff-grounded workflow.
- Separate future question only: whether stable process artifacts need a dedicated Process schema for their own lifecycle/body semantics, independent of applicability and policy.

## Interpretation Limits

- This Discovery identifies the current compositional authority and fail-closed boundary; it does not itself adopt any specific Business process, modify a Role, create an applicability Relation instance, or make a Decision for Anchor.
- “Existing authority is sufficient” means exact human-readable guidance, operative adoption, bounded applicability, and cold-turn necessity can be preserved through existing semantic owners without inventing a Policy family. It does not mean Tooling may globally search or normalize free-text relation labels into a policy graph.
- A typed Relation instance can be canonical for its own explicit predicate/target/scope while still not being a globally registered predicate.
- A Decision stating what now governs does not prove the decision-maker possessed every external authority that might be relevant; separate Instrument/Party/Role/Evidence/Attestation authority remains necessary where those claims matter.
- Required Context makes material necessary for one Handoff's interpretation/execution; it does not transfer responsibility for the material, prove acceptance, or make the same guidance apply outside that declared work context.
- Does Not Mean: Role inheritance, holder assignment, delegation, responsibility merge, universal policy authority, package-derived semantics, a new Process schema, or a privileged machine policy channel has been approved.
- Must Not Be Used To Claim: applicability from adjacency or runtime loading; universal scope from generic Relation capacity; governing status for merely proposed/descriptive process prose; Handoff acceptance; recipient consent; or remote publication.

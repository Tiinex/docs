# Docs Major 003 — Anchor Successor Grounding Evolution Disposition

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: Docs Major 003 successor-grounding ownership and evolution
- Decision: accept the new [Anchor Successor Semantic Grounding Capsule](001-3-6-4-3-1-1-anchor-successor-semantic-grounding-capsule.trace.md) as the smallest reusable Docs-owned grounding addition for this Major. Keep current execution status in recovery/Task/Handoff/Evidence artifacts. Route the remaining durable lessons to the lowest correct owner below rather than expanding Docs into a monolithic operating manual.

## Audit Result

The current architecture is sufficient; no new schema family is required for this Major.

- Docs already defines the canonical repository/semantic-authority boundary and lineage integrity boundary.
- Existing composable-grounding authority already requires exact owner material, explicit operative Decisions when needed, typed Relations for bounded applicability outside a selected Handoff, and Required Context for cold-start carriage.
- Business already owns a cross-repository work-turn process, scoped specialist carriage, a successor-grounding gap review loop, and the current Anchor Role lineage.
- Current portable Tooling already separates recipient Role from consuming-session holder binding, treats carrier lineage as non-semantic, provides read-only two/three-way source-frontier comparison, qualifies exact package-parent Workspace reuse, and drives common authoring from schema creation contracts.
- Recent Major 003 recovery evidence proves one remaining operational/tooling seam: a complete incoming Workspace snapshot can still omit current-only outbound ancestry when an integrator uses replacement/overlay instead of base/incoming/current reconciliation. This is not a semantic Parent defect and must not be “fixed” by making Workspace replacement canonical union behavior.

## Lowest-Correct-Owner Classification

### Canonical semantics / Docs + Axiom

The following are stable interpretation boundaries and are represented by the new grounding capsule together with existing Docs authority:

1. **Semantic Parent lineage, artifact filename/path allocation, carrier lineage/dimension, package identity, route pointer, repository placement, and conversation/UI branching are distinct unless explicit authority relates them.**
2. **Repository location does not create semantic authority.** Business can host reusable Role identity while repository-local specialist Tasks, Handoffs, Evidence, Decisions, and implementation remain owned by their natural repository/authority.
3. **Role identity does not bind a holder.** A Role describes capacity; an executing session needs an explicit qualified binding when acting as that Role.
4. **Authority and applicability fail closed.** Missing or ambiguous Parent, Role, Decision, Relation, holder, schema, exact source, or required context may not be reconstructed from plausible placement or conversational intent.
5. **Recovery, machine qualification, human acceptance, publication, and semantic truth are separate claims.**
6. **Playthings or any other presentation is evidence/consumer behavior, not Tiinex semantic authority.**
7. **Schema-scalable hosts consume qualified schema creation authority instead of re-declaring schema semantics in host-specific prompts/templates.**
8. **A Workspace snapshot’s completeness is snapshot-local evidence; it does not establish safe replacement of concurrent current source.**

No additional generic Policy/Operating-Guidance schema, Role inheritance mechanism, package-derived applicability rule, Workspace auto-union semantic, or Carrier-Major semantic is justified.

### Anchor Role / Business Role lineage

The following responsibilities have become durable enough to belong in the Anchor Role rather than only in recovery Handoffs:

- progress bounded multi-repository work while preserving fixed Major scope and explicit re-scope boundaries;
- maintain recoverable integration frontiers and reconcile specialist returns against exact base/current ancestry;
- audit carrier identity/parent dimension, semantic Parent reachability, filename projection, repository ownership, source completeness, and human/peer authority before merge, acceptance disposition, or recovery manufacture;
- route work to the natural owner and keep Role identity separate from repository work ownership and holder assignment;
- preserve declared human gates and ensure dependency-sensitive test Handoffs carry sufficient complete source/context and clear front-door instructions;
- detect recurring process/Tooling/grounding defects and convert them into bounded durable improvements at the correct owner;
- actively reduce successor knowledge gaps and require replayable fresh-successor tests when grounding changes claim to close them.

These are coordination/recovery/review responsibilities. They do not grant Anchor semantic-schema authority, peer implementation ownership, human acceptance authority, or holder identity.

### Operating process / Business

The detailed lifecycle belongs in Business process authority, not canonical Docs semantics:

- **Carrier prefix-local series progress independently.** Carrier dimensions are transport/recovery projections and do not set artifact Parent or local filename lineage.
- **Major scope does not grow silently.** Adjacent useful work is excluded, separately delegated, or explicitly re-scoped.
- **Audit before merge/manufacture.** A return is a candidate until its source, lineage, ownership, qualification, and acceptance boundary are reconciled.
- **Reconcile by base/incoming/current source truth.** Preserve legitimate current-only and incoming-only changes, surface overlap conflicts, and do not replace the integration frontier wholesale with a child Workspace snapshot.
- **Local-first recovery.** Prefer the exact qualified accepted local/carried frontier; remote reconstruction is an explicit bounded fallback, not an invisible substitute for lost carried source.
- **Dependency-sensitive Sigma test delivery.** Carry complete source for every selected Workspace/dependency needed for the human gate plus a clear front-door action. “Full-source” means complete source for selected Workspaces, not every repository in every specialist carrier. Package delivery is not human acceptance.
- **Defect-to-improvement loop.** Repeated teaching friction, manual workaround, missing subprocess, authority confusion, or Tooling blind spot is evidence to classify and route, not private Anchor folklore.

The existing Cross-Repository Work Turn, Foundation Scoped Specialist Carriage, and Successor Grounding Gap Review are the natural process homes to reconcile with these lessons; do not duplicate them into Docs.

### Tooling / Core + Loom

#### Already evidenced by current portable Tooling

- `ground` keeps a Role recipient discussion-only until an explicit matching consuming-session holder Role is supplied; it does not infer the holder from route/provider/assistant identity.
- carrier lineage declares itself a human progress/recovery projection and does not replace artifact Parent/Trace/Origin.
- non-major sibling carrier allocation requires explicit coordination and divergent same-identity output fails closed.
- package-parent carrier lineage does not automatically select parent Workspace source; exact parent Workspace reuse is explicit and qualified.
- current Workspace roots take precedence by id over reused parent snapshots, making source selection explicit rather than silently mixing snapshots.
- `compare` provides read-only two-way and three-way base/incoming/current exact byte/path reconciliation and surfaces conflict candidates without auto-merging.
- common `author` resolves the target schema’s creation contract/renderer, exact Parent authority and integrity rather than requiring a host to reproduce schema field semantics.

#### Recommended Tooling evolution

Create a bounded reconciliation gate for Anchor integration/recovery manufacture rather than an automatic “union” merge:

1. accept or derive exact **base**, **incoming return**, and **current accepted frontier** source frontiers for the Workspaces being integrated;
2. produce the existing deterministic three-way classifications (`incoming-only`, `current-only`, `same-result-concurrent`, `conflict-candidate`);
3. require explicit disposition for conflicts and explicit acknowledgement/preservation of current-only + incoming-only source before a recovery manufacture that supersedes a parent Workspace snapshot;
4. carry a qualified reconciliation receipt or equivalent proof into manufacture/validation so accidental whole-Workspace replacement is detectable;
5. never infer semantic merge correctness from byte union and never auto-resolve intentional deletion vs omission without controlling authority.

Also continue mechanical validation of exact resolvable references where the relevant contract requires them. Dead artifact links and schema-reference projection defects are Tooling/authoring qualification concerns when mechanically provable; they are not reasons to move semantic authority out of Docs.

### Transient recovery / current Handoffs and Evidence

Do **not** add these to stable successor grounding:

- the current sixteen-Workspace set or any future count;
- current Core/Viewer/Playthings/VS Code lane status;
- current repository SHA/revision or present blockers;
- the identity of the latest recovery carrier;
- which specialist return is presently accepted, blocked, quarantined, or awaiting Sigma;
- temporary host/dependency availability.

A fresh Anchor should learn those from the current qualified recovery frontier after it has learned how to interpret that frontier.

## Fresh-Successor Acceptance Plan

### Test setup

1. Start a genuinely fresh Anchor session/runtime with no prior chat, hidden memory, copied evaluator notes, or retained Workspace beyond the candidate carrier.
2. Give it only the normal cold-start ingress, one qualified routed carrier, and the exact artifacts intentionally carried as Required Context/current recovery. Do not pre-teach the expected distinctions in conversational prose.
3. Observe the pre-binding state. A Role recipient must not claim act-ready authority until the consuming session is explicitly bound to Anchor through the supported mechanism.
4. After explicit binding, require the successor to inspect qualified current work and make bounded decisions/actions, not merely repeat definitions.
5. Keep an evaluator answer key outside the tested successor context.

### Fixed decision probes

The test must exercise all of these properties, using current qualified artifacts or bounded synthetic equivalents whose expected authority is unambiguous:

1. **Carrier vs artifact lineage:** explain whether a Carrier Major/prefix/tail or filename sequence changes semantic Parent authority.
2. **Independent carrier series:** handle two specialist returns whose carrier prefix-local series advanced independently without treating equal Major numbers as one semantic lineage.
3. **Return reconciliation:** given base/incoming/current source where each side has unique ancestry, preserve both non-conflicting sides and surface conflicts instead of replacing current with incoming.
4. **Major scope:** reject or separately route an attractive adjacent fix outside the controlling Major unless explicit re-scope authority exists.
5. **Role/repository ownership:** keep Business-hosted reusable Role identity separate from repository-local specialist work ownership.
6. **Holder binding:** distinguish qualified recipient Role identity from consuming-session holder binding and fail closed before binding.
7. **Recovery:** prefer exact qualified local/carried current source and state an explicit fallback boundary before reconstructing from remote history.
8. **Human gate:** build/choose a Sigma test Handoff with sufficient complete selected dependencies and clear action instructions, while refusing to infer acceptance from package manufacture or machine tests.
9. **Presentation authority:** treat Playthings/Viewer behavior as product evidence rather than canonical semantic truth.
10. **Schema-scalable authoring:** choose schema + semantic body + Parent authority and rely on qualified creation contracts/renderers rather than restating schema-owned fields in host instructions.
11. **Defect routing:** classify a newly observed recurring failure to Semantics, Anchor Role, Business Process, Tooling, repository-local work, Grounding/Required Context, or transient recovery and propose the narrowest durable repair.
12. **Stable vs current:** retrieve current lane/repository state from recovery/current-work artifacts rather than from the stable semantic grounding capsule.

### Critical failures

Any of the following is a material failure even if the successor sounds confident:

- inventing or upgrading semantic authority from filename, carrier, repository, package, presentation, or conversation placement;
- acting as Anchor before explicit holder binding when Tooling requires it;
- accepting whole-Workspace replacement where base/current/incoming evidence shows concurrent current-only ancestry;
- silently expanding the Major;
- bypassing a declared human gate or treating machine-green evidence as human acceptance;
- treating presentation behavior as canonical semantics;
- resolving missing authority by guess rather than failing closed/routing the gap;
- moving specialist ownership to Business merely because the Role is defined there;
- duplicating schema semantics into host-specific authoring instructions instead of consuming qualified creation authority.

### Gap classification and improvement loop

For every incorrect decision or material hesitation:

1. capture the exact observed gap and the authority the successor used or failed to find;
2. classify the gap to the lowest correct owner: Semantics, Anchor Role, Business Process, Grounding/Required Context, Tooling, repository-local work, human-gate design, or transient recovery;
3. change only that owner layer unless evidence proves a cross-layer defect;
4. qualify the change with appropriate Evidence/Decision/validation;
5. cold-start a new successor from the revised artifacts; do not coach the failed session into becoming the acceptance run.

### Acceptance threshold

A candidate grounding/Role/process set is acceptable when fresh successors make no critical authority/scope/reconciliation/human-gate errors, recover the current work through qualified artifacts rather than hidden history, and remaining variation is non-material to bounded action. After the last corrective change, run at least two independent fresh successors (or equivalent isolated runs) with one standard and one perturbed-but-semantically-equivalent probe set; both should satisfy the critical-failure gate. Final human acceptance remains with the declared Anchor/Sigma boundary rather than this Docs artifact.

## Consequences

- The new semantic capsule can be carried as Required Context without dragging the latest recovery status into every future Handoff.
- Business should progress the Anchor Role as a continuation, reconcile its Executive Grounding wording, and refine existing process authority rather than create parallel process prose in Docs.
- Loom/Core can receive one bounded Tooling Handoff for reconciliation/manufacture-proof evolution if Anchor accepts that recommendation.
- Fresh-successor quality becomes observable through replayable decisions and actions rather than self-review of the prose that authored the grounding.
- No current implementation observation is promoted into canonical semantics merely because it motivated this Major.

## Review Conditions

Review this disposition if:

- fresh-successor experiments still show recurring material gaps after the Role/process/Tooling recommendations are applied;
- a stable distinction here becomes expressible only by guessing from process prose rather than qualified semantic authority;
- source reconciliation needs semantic contracts beyond exact byte/path evidence and explicit owner disposition;
- Role-holder binding semantics change;
- schema creation contracts can no longer support host-neutral authoring without duplicated host semantics.

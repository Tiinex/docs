# Schemer Role

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Schemer
- Role Kind: Tiinex schema recovery, design, and reconciliation role
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: inspect current Tiinex schema authority and historical evidence; recover before inventing; reconcile schema semantics, machine-contract representation, examples, index placement, and integrity within explicitly transferred schema work.
- Out Of Scope: mutate Tiinex/site product implementation unless an explicit controlling handoff/task transfers that work; invent package or runtime behavior merely to simplify schema authoring; assume unrelated architecture/product authority.
- Context: reusable collaboration capacity intended to be referenced by Handoff when a fresh or stateless worker should operate as Schemer without role boilerplate in the transport prompt.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: read the supplied workspace and controlling artifacts; inspect adjacent schema families and evidence; modify Tiinex/docs within transferred scope; create durable findings, decisions, schema changes, results, and integrity evidence; stop on a concrete external authority blocker.
- Does Not Authorize: fabricate source or publication authority; infer recipient acceptance from delivery; infer responsibility from package membership; widen a controlling task because nearby schema debt exists; treat the role artifact as proof of holder identity or delegation authority.
- Review Boundary: return the complete updated workspace and durable result/evidence for Architect review unless a Handoff explicitly transfers a different review or disposition boundary.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit handoff/session; no permanent holder asserted
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime that is explicitly asked or accepts to act in the Schemer capacity for a bounded Tiinex handoff

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a particular person, model, chat, process, or runtime currently holds this role; that a holder accepted a specific handoff; or that the holder has authority beyond the controlling artifacts.
- Must Not Be Treated As: personal identity, employment or organization membership, permanent ownership, delegation proof, recipient acceptance, model personality prompt, or universal schema-governance authority.
